LEARNING LAB

AI Lab

A place for agents to learn how to navigate worlds you create.

TRAINING SETUP

AGENT PERCEPTION

A heading-relative 9 × 9 local view of walls, movable cargo, lava, timed lasers, electric gates, ice, power-ups, plates, levers, coins, moving obstacles, switches, and doors, plus four predicted danger layers for rotating bars, timed laser sweeps, and electric gates. A shortest-path compass says which neighbouring cells are closer to the current goal (plate, then exit), and the level timer is included. Absolute coordinates are not supplied, so the policy generalises across levels.

ALGORITHM HYPERPARAMETERS
REWARD CONFIGURATION

“Progress” is potential-based shaping on the true shortest-path distance to the current goal, so it speeds up learning without changing which behaviour is best. Every episode starts on a random level with a random bar phase; finishing with more time left earns a larger speed bonus. Values are saved in the checkpoint.
